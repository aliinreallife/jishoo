# Story layout correction — 17 October 2026

The 0.2.3 composition reduced the mascots and statistics poster too far. The correction restores the earlier 840/920px illustration widths, larger droplet grids and a wider statistics poster. Backgrounds remain full bleed. Only necessary positions and text budgets are adjusted to fit the portrait.

The provisional design allowance is **250px at both ends of a 1080×1920 portrait**, with 80px side gutters. This replaces the earlier 340px bottom allowance. It is a conventional design guideline, not a measured Instagram interface or a promise about every device. Square layouts retain their separate arrangement.

Current-source verification was attempted against:

- [Meta's Instagram Stories image guide](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/instagram-story)
- [Hootsuite's Instagram Story dimensions guide](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-story-dimensions/)
- [Adobe Express Story dimensions](https://www.adobe.com/express/discover/sizes/instagram/story)
- [Buffer's Story size guide](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-story-size/)
- Google and Bing searches for organic Story safe areas

The execution environment returned network-level **HTTP 403** for all these pages, including after network permission was granted. No current quotation or official numerical recommendation was verified. Advertising placements and organic Story controls must not be treated as identical guidance. The 250px allowance remains explicitly provisional until a current source or real-device measurements can be checked.

The browser test measures actual canvas text and bitmap bounds against the chosen allowance, checks text collisions, and creates simulated account/reply overlays for visual comparison. The overlay images are QA simulations, not screenshots of Instagram.
